# MOTION

## Principles

Motion should support understanding.

Never distract.

Never delay gameplay.

Every animation has a purpose.

---

# Screen Transitions

Fast.

Smooth.

Natural.

Never flashy.

---

# Buttons

Small scale animation on press.

Immediate response.

---

# Cards

Soft appearance animation.

Subtle lift when selected.

---

# Countdown

Clear rhythm.

Large animated numbers.

Strong anticipation before gameplay.

---

# Gameplay

Word changes instantly.

Tilt feedback is immediate.

Brief success or failure flash.

Short haptic confirmation.

No unnecessary animations during gameplay.

---

# Mimik

Expressive.

Playful.

Exaggerated reactions.

Never interrupts gameplay.

Appears mainly before and after turns.

---

# Success

Quick celebration.

Positive motion.

Confetti reserved for meaningful achievements.

---

# Error

Short shake.

Brief red flash.

Immediate recovery.

---

# Timing

Interactions should feel responsive.

Animations should never make players wait.

Gameplay always has priority over visual effects.
