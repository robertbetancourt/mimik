# SOUND_SYSTEM

## Principles

Sounds reinforce actions.

Keep them short.

Never become distracting.

Haptic feedback should complement important sounds.

---

# UI

Button

Soft click.

Back

Soft tap.

Selection

Positive click.

---

# Gameplay

Countdown

Three short ticks.

Start

Energetic "Go!"

Correct

Short positive confirmation.

Pass

Soft negative confirmation.

Time Up

Clear finish sound.

---

# Results

Winner

Celebration.

Play Again

Positive confirmation.

---

# General Rules

No background music during gameplay.

Sound effects should never overlap excessively.

Every important gameplay action should have a matching haptic response.
