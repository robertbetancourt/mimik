# ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

## Goal

Build a modern, maintainable and scalable codebase.

Choose the simplest solution that satisfies the product requirements.

---

## Principles

Prefer official Expo libraries.

Prefer actively maintained packages.

Avoid unnecessary dependencies.

Prioritize readability over cleverness.

Favor composition over inheritance.

Keep components small and reusable.

Avoid premature optimization.

Minimize boilerplate.

---

## Architecture

The implementation is intentionally left flexible.

The engineer may choose modern libraries and patterns when they improve maintainability, developer experience or performance.

---

## Performance

Animations should remain smooth.

Gameplay responsiveness has priority over visual effects.

Avoid unnecessary re-renders.

---

## Future

The project should remain easy to extend with:

- new categories
- new game modes
- new animations
- new languages
