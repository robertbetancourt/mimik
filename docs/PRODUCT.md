# PRODUCT

## Name

Mimik

---

## Overview

Premium local party game.

Charades experience.

One shared phone.

Offline first.

Designed for families and friends.

---

## Mission

Create the most polished local charades experience available on mobile.

Prioritize quality over quantity.

---

## Vision

People should spend their time laughing together, not navigating menus.

The app should disappear so the game can shine.

---

## Audience

- Families
- Friends
- Couples
- Small gatherings
- Players of all ages

---

## Experience

- Fast to start
- Easy to learn
- Fun to replay
- Offline
- Shared around one phone

---

## Core Principles

- Simplicity first
- Premium craftsmanship
- Play before configuration
- Family friendly
- Personality over gimmicks
- Quality over features

---

## Product Pillars

### Social

Built for playing together in the same room.

---

### Offline

No internet.

No accounts.

No servers.

Play anywhere.

---

### Premium

Clean interface.

Editorial composition.

High-quality illustrations.

Carefully crafted motion.

---

### Playful

Humor through reactions.

Small delightful moments.

Expressive animations.

Never childish.

---

## Mimik

Role:

Silent spectator.

Never speaks.

Communicates only through:

- Expressions
- Body language
- Reactions

Personality:

- Playful
- Curious
- Dramatic
- Mischievous
- Warm
- Expressive
- Slightly impatient

Mimik reacts to the game.

Never chooses favorites.

Never becomes mean.

---

## Success Criteria

Players should:

- Understand the game in seconds.
- Laugh frequently.
- Want another match immediately.
- Feel the experience is polished.
- Enjoy playing without ads or interruptions.

---

## MVP Goal

Deliver a complete and polished version ready for real family gatherings before July 31.
