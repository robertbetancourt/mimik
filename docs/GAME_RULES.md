# GAME_RULES

## Match

A match consists of one or more rounds.

Players configure the match before starting.

The match ends when:

- All configured rounds are completed.
- Players manually end the match.

---

## Match Configuration

Players choose:

- Category
- Number of players
- Round duration
- Number of rounds

Or

- Continue until manually ended.

---

## Turn Order

- Every player plays once per round.
- Turn order remains the same throughout the match.

---

## Turn Flow

1. Show current player.
2. Countdown.
3. Phone orientation check.
4. Show first word.
5. Start timer.
6. Gameplay.
7. Turn summary.
8. Next player.

---

## Countdown

Sequence:

3

2

1

Prepare!

The game starts only after the phone is in the correct playing position.

---

## Phone Position

Phone must be:

- Vertical
- Screen facing outward
- Top edge pointing upward

If orientation is incorrect:

Show:

"Place the phone on your forehead."

The timer starts only after the correct position is detected and the first word is displayed.

---

## Gameplay

Visible during gameplay:

- Current word
- Remaining time

Nothing else.

No buttons.

No score.

No progress.

Players interact only by tilting the phone.

---

## Controls

Tilt Down

→ Correct

Tilt Up

→ Pass

Every action triggers:

- Haptic feedback
- Visual feedback
- Short input cooldown
- Next word

---

## Correct Word

Actions:

- Green screen
- "Correct"
- +1 point
- Load next word

---

## Passed Word

Actions:

- Red screen
- "Pass"
- No points
- Load next word

---

## Words

Words are randomly shuffled at the beginning of every turn.

Words never repeat during the same turn.

Passed words are not shown again during that turn.

---

## Timer

The timer starts when the first word appears.

When the timer reaches zero:

- Finish current turn.
- Show turn summary.

---

## Turn Summary

Display:

- Player name
- Score
- Correct words
- Passed words
- Mimik reaction
- Next player

---

## Round

A round ends after every player has completed one turn.

If more rounds remain:

Start next round.

Otherwise:

Finish match.

---

## Final Results

Display:

- Winner
- Final ranking
- Celebration
- Play Again
- Return Home

---

## Scoring

Correct

+1 point

Pass

0 points

No penalties.

---

## Tie

Players with the same score share the same position.

No tiebreaker.

---

## Finish Match

Players may manually finish the match at any time.

Current scores become the final results.

---

## Offline

Entire gameplay works offline.

No internet connection required.

No external services.

---

## Game Philosophy

Fast.

Simple.

Readable.

The player should focus on acting.

Everyone else should focus on guessing.

The interface should never distract from the game.
